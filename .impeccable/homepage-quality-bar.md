# Homepage quality bar

The user selected Read.cv and Bento.me as the craft references for the intentionally conventional personal-homepage direction.

- References: https://read.cv/ and https://bento.me/
- Frame: restrained maximum width, immediate identity, and obvious destinations without a dashboard shell.
- Depth: shallow, soft elevation used only to separate actionable surfaces.
- Lettering: highly legible sans-serif hierarchy with decisive display weight and comfortable Chinese body measure.
- Ornament density: low; whitespace, alignment, and scale do the work.
- Motion: brief entrance choreography and direct interaction feedback only.
- Responsive behavior: desktop hierarchy collapses to one readable mobile column without horizontal scrolling.

These references set finish quality, not branding. Do not copy their logos, proprietary visuals, exact layouts, or blue/purple treatments. The repository's product constraints and direction contract remain authoritative.
